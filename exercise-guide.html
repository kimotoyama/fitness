Strength & Mobility Exercise Guide

A companion to the workout checklist. Use this page for form reminders, movement cues, and the added band/ring work. Strength reps should generally be controlled: about 2–3 seconds down and 1–2 seconds up, with no need to rush between exercises.

Progression rule: once you reach the top of the rep range with good form and 1–3 reps still in reserve, make the movement harder rather than simply adding endless reps.
Stop any movement that causes sharp pain, numbness, or joint pain. Mild muscular effort and stretching discomfort are expected; pain is not.
